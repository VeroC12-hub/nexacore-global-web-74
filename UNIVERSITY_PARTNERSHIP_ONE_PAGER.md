### **OUR UNIVERSITY PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM**

**Vision:** To position NexaCore as the leading industry-academic partner in Africa, integrating digital engineering and modern technologies into higher education.

**Mission:** To empower universities to produce industry-ready graduates through practical training, research collaboration, and faculty development.

---

### **3 ENGAGEMENT STREAMS**

#### **STREAM 1: Digital Engineering Education (DEE) Series**
- Technical seminars and guest lectures
- Live demonstrations and workshops
- Faculty and student awareness programs
- 200-500 participants per session

#### **STREAM 2: Co-Branded Technical Training & Certification**
- CAD/BIM/Design (AutoCAD, Revit, SolidWorks)
- Process Optimization & Lean Six Sigma
- MATLAB, Python, AI for Engineers
- Project Management tools
- Short courses (1-2 weeks) to semester-long programs

#### **STREAM 3: Research, Innovation & Industrial Collaboration**
- NexaCore Innovation Hubs on campus
- Capstone & thesis projects with industry data
- Joint research grants
- Industrial visits and internships
- Mentorship programs
