## 🎨 Quick Design Tips

### Using Canva (Free Tool)
```
1. Go to canva.com
2. Create free account
3. Use templates for:
   - LinkedIn posts (1200 x 1200)
   - Instagram posts (1080 x 1080)
   - Facebook covers (820 x 312)
   - Twitter headers (1500 x 500)
4. Add your logo and brand colors
5. Download as PNG or JPG
```
