**RC Engineering Limited — Structural & Civil Engineering (Ghana)**
Migrated from manual AutoCAD RC detailing to an automated BIM workflow (Revit + AutoRebar + CADS RC), with custom templates to Ghanaian & British standards (BS 8110) and hands-on training.
**Results:** 50% less detailing/scheduling time · 90%+ reinforcement-schedule accuracy · standardized templates · 8 engineers/draftsmen trained.
*"The NexaCore team helped us transition from manual RC detailing to a fully automated Revit and AutoRebar workflow. This has transformed our documentation speed, accuracy, and presentation." — Managing Director, RC Engineering Ltd.*

**FCL Processing Limited — Chemical / Mineral Processing (Ghana)**
Transitioned from 2D AutoCAD to a model-based environment (AutoCAD Plant 3D + Navisworks): full 3D plant model, intelligent P&IDs, layout optimization, automated isometrics/BOMs, engineer training — in ~6 weeks.
**Results:** 40% less layout rework · ~30% faster drawing/review cycles · fully digitized as-built documentation.
*"The NexaCore team helped us see our plant in 3D for the first time. Their approach simplified our review process and improved coordination between departments." — Engineering Manager, FCL Processing Ltd.*

**FCL Ghana Limited — Cocoa Processing (Ghana)**
Designed and documented a new cocoa-processing facility in Plant 3D + Navisworks: full 3D model (conveyors, silos, piping), equipment layouts (roasting/grinding/packaging), P&IDs, isometrics & BOMs, energy-efficiency recommendations.
**Results:** fully coordinated 3D plant model delivered (Jan–Sep 2024) · ~35% better equipment arrangement & process-flow efficiency · reduced rework and contractor communication gaps.
